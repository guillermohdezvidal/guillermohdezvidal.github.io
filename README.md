# Guillermo Hernández Vidal · Research

Source of **<https://guillermohdezvidal.github.io>**: a static, bilingual (English/Spanish) site with small,
pre-registered research projects in quantitative finance. Each project has its paper (PDF) and an interactive
dashboard whose charts are computed in the visitor's browser from a small SQLite database (sql.js + Plotly).
No server, no cookies, no analytics.

## Projects

- **Same models, different conditions.** Why a quant fund's methods need not give a retail trader its results:
  16 pre-registered tests of pairs trading, Avellaneda–Lee residuals and funding carry on crypto perpetuals under
  MiFID II (2022–2026). None passed; the dashboard and the paper show why.
  [Dashboard](https://guillermohdezvidal.github.io/proyectos/arbitraje-estadistico/) ·
  [Paper (PDF)](https://guillermohdezvidal.github.io/proyectos/arbitraje-estadistico/articulo.pdf)

## Data

The databases contain only backtest results and weekly aggregated measures. No prices or raw market data from any
exchange are distributed.
